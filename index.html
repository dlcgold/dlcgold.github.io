About

My research focuses on the design and implementation of efficient algorithms and data structures for bioinformatics and computational biology, optimizing for both time and memory. I work at the intersection of theoretical results, such as succinct and compressed data structures and pattern-matching algorithms, and practical, large-scale genomic data.

During my Ph.D. at the BIAS research laboratory (Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca), I developed the μ-PBWT, a lightweight r-index for haplotype panels enabling maximal-match queries in sublinear space on datasets like the UK Biobank. I extended this work to genotype phasing, the Minimal Positional Substring Cover problem, and pangenome graph indexing with gindex.

I've collaborated internationally with research groups at Dalhousie University, the University of Florida, Johns Hopkins, the University of Tokyo, the University of Cambridge, Illumina, and the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland, including secondments in Tokyo, Cambridge and Helsinki as part of the EU Horizon 2020 PANGAIA project.
